About Dr. Rajini Muthineni

Best Gynecologist in Hyderabad

Dr. Muthineni Rajini is a highly trusted Obstetrician & Gynecologist in Hyderabad with over 18+ years of clinical excellence in women’s healthcare. She specializes in managing high-risk pregnancies, infertility treatments, and advanced gynecological procedures including laparoscopic, hysteroscopic, and robotic surgeries.

Known for her calm and patient-first approach, Dr. Rajini combines advanced medical technology with compassionate care to deliver safe outcomes and personalized maternity solutions. She is widely recognized as a Top Gynecologist in Hyderabad for handling complex cases with confidence and precision.

Comprehensive Women’s Care
Dr. Rajini provides complete healthcare support for women at every stage of life, from adolescence to menopause. Her services include high-risk and routine pregnancy care, natural and assisted deliveries, infertility evaluation and treatment, hormonal disorder management, advanced laparoscopic and robotic surgeries, and cosmetic gynecology.

Experience & Expertise
Currently practicing at Care Hospitals, Banjara Hills and Ikigai Clinics, Kondapur, Dr. Rajini brings decades of hands-on experience as a senior obstetrician, gynecologist, and minimally invasive surgeon. Her expertise in modern laparoscopic techniques allows her to effectively manage complex gynecological conditions and infertility challenges with excellent results.
